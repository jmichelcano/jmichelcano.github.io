About me

I'm an aerospace engineering student at Wichita State University and the first in my family to go to college. I've spent four summers on Northrop Grumman's manufacturing process engineering team, and I now write structural substantiation for FAA certification at J.B. Dwerlkotte Associates.

My experience is in manufacturing, hands-on builds, structural analysis, and continuous improvement. Next, I want to work closer to the finished aircraft: flight test, survivability, and RCS analysis.
