## The problem

A local elementary school had a mock F-35 cockpit, donated by the Kansas Aviation Museum, with an old simulator inside that no longer worked.

## What we did

I worked with a small volunteer team to rebuild it into a working simulator:

- Removed the parts of the old simulator that were no longer needed.
- Installed a new PC that runs X-Plane 12.
- Mounted a 55-inch main display in front of the cockpit and a second screen as an instructor station.
- Set up a HOTAS throttle and sidestick plus rudder pedals.
- Added console panels, guarded switches, interior LED lighting, and surround-sound speakers so the cockpit looks and sounds closer to the real thing.

## Result

Students can now fly everything from a Cessna 172 to a fighter jet. From the instructor station, a teacher picks the aircraft and airport and changes the weather, time of day, and system failures without stopping the simulator.
