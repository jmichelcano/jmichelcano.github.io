## The problem

A local elementary school had a mock F-35 cockpit, donated by the Kansas Aviation Museum, with an old simulator inside that no longer worked. <!-- CONFIRM museum name -->

## What I did

- Removed the parts of the old simulator that were no longer needed.
- Installed a new PC capable of running X-Plane 12.
- Mounted a 55-inch display in the cockpit.
- Set up Thrustmaster throttle, sidestick, and rudder pedals.
- Added interior LED lighting and surround-sound speakers so the cockpit looks and sounds closer to the real thing.
