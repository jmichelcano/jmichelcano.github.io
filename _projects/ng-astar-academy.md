## The program

ASTAR Academy is a week-long hands-on course at Northrop Grumman in Palmdale. Each trainee starts with a bare composite panel and takes it through the same manufacturing steps used on the aircraft: locating holes, drilling, reaming, sanding the composite, installing fasteners, and sealing them.

## What I did

I joined as a trainee and built my own panel. The program had a photographer assigned to capture the process, but after the first day he was pulled onto another project. I was the only other person approved to film, so I took over while still finishing my panel.

- Photographed trainees at each step of the build.
- Recorded testimonial videos of trainees at the end of the week. These were shared inside the company.
- Expanded the coverage to document the wider intern experience.
- Worked with the media team to get the photos approved for public release. It was the first time interns could take home approved photos of their own work.

## Result

A few weeks later, a company VP heard about the program and asked our team to present it and its results. The presentation was a team effort, and I presented my part of it. Since then, the program has expanded from mechanics to almost all new employees, including engineers, managers, and VPs, so they understand what a mechanic goes through to build the product.

*All photos on this page were approved for public release by Northrop Grumman's media team.*
