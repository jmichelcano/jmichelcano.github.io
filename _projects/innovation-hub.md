## The Hub

Wichita State's Project Innovation Hub is an open makerspace where students build class projects, competition robots, and personal ideas. As a lab mentor, I kept the machines running and helped students get from a CAD file to a finished part safely.

## What I did

- Trained students on the CNC plasma cutter, waterjet, laser cutter, CNC wood mills, sandblasters, and 3D printers.
- Helped students with Fusion 360 CAD, 3D-print slicing, and CNC programming.
- Promoted safe machine use, so student projects could get built without injuries or damaged equipment.
- Helped build, troubleshoot, and repair projects and equipment around the shop.
