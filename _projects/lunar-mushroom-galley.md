## The challenge

A long-duration lunar habitat has to produce some of its own food with limited power, volume, and crew time. Our team proposed a compact galley that grows mushrooms in a closed loop.

## What we designed

The concept was a team effort. The design work covered:

- **Power estimates** for lighting and control electronics.
- **Nutrient estimates** for the food the mushrooms produce.
- **System layout**: vertical growing columns, a removable tray, and integrated grow lighting.
- **Energy use and efficiency** of the whole unit.
- **Ease of use and transport**, so a crew could operate it and move it as a single unit.

## My part

On a team of five, I built the CAD model of the full system and then built the working prototype. I also supplied the Arduino and electronics that bring it to life: a temperature and humidity sensor, LED grow lights, and an LCD and keypad.

## Result

We took **first place** at the 2025 Innovation Works Challenge, sponsored by Lockheed Martin Skunk Works.
